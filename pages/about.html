The project

About Taste of Ethiopia

A digital collection that brings Ethiopian recipes, food culture and visual stories together in one welcoming place.

Why this website?

Taste of Ethiopia brings recipes, food photography and a little cultural context together in one place. It is designed to be simple to explore and easy to keep improving.

About the Creator

This website was designed and developed by Sofanit Kibrom as part of the She Plus Tech – Code the Future Program. It is my first website project, created to explore Ethiopian food, culture, and traditions while developing my web development skills.

Built around food

The site combines recipe pages, category browsing, a visual gallery, an Ethiopia information page and a simple idea-sharing form.

Technologies Used

This website was built using HTML for the structure, CSS for the design and styling, and JavaScript for interactive features and functionality.

What is included?

Visitors can browse recipes by category, explore Ethiopian food beyond the recipe collection, learn a little about Ethiopia and suggest ideas for future additions.
