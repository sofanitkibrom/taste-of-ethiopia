Vegetarian / Mixed

Beyaynetu

A colorful shared platter rather than one single recipe: several Ethiopian dishes are arranged over injera so diners can combine different flavors in one meal.

⏱ 45–90 mins
👥 4 servings
← All Recipes

🛒 Ingredients

  • 4–6 sheets fresh injera
  • Misir Wat
  • Gomen
  • Kik Alicha
  • Shiro
  • Keysir or another beet salad
  • Optional additional vegetable dishes

🍳 Step-by-Step

  1. Choose a mixture of dishes with different colors, textures and spice levels. A good platter balances spicy foods such as Misir Wat with mild dishes such as Kik Alicha or Gomen.
  2. Prepare each component separately. Keep stews warm and prepare salads or cold sides shortly before serving.
  3. Spread a fresh sheet of injera over a large platter, leaving the edges visible.
  4. Spoon the different dishes into separate sections around the injera. Arrange the colors so the platter looks varied and inviting.
  5. Add small garnishes such as fresh chili, herbs or a salad if appropriate. Avoid overcrowding the platter so each dish remains distinct.
  6. Serve communally. Tear pieces of injera from the edge, use them to pick up the different foods, and enjoy combinations of spicy, mild, sour and savory flavors.
Kitchen note: Taste and adjust salt and spice gradually. Ethiopian dishes can vary by household and region, so the balance of heat, richness and acidity can be personalized.
