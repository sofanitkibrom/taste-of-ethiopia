Dinich Wat

A comforting Ethiopian potato stew with soft potatoes coated in an aromatic onion, garlic and berbere sauce.

⏱ 40–50 mins
👥 4 servings
← All Recipes

🛒 Ingredients

  • 4 large potatoes, peeled and cut into chunks
  • 2 large red onions, finely chopped
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil or niter kibbeh
  • 1–2 tbsp berbere
  • 1 tbsp garlic paste
  • 1 tsp ginger paste
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • 2 cups water or vegetable stock
  • Salt to taste

🍳 Step-by-Step

  1. Peel the potatoes and cut them into evenly sized chunks so they cook at the same rate. Keep them in water while preparing the sauce.
  2. Cook the onions in a heavy pot without oil until soft and reduced. Add the oil or niter kibbeh and cook for another few minutes.
  3. Add garlic, ginger, berbere and tomato paste. Stir over medium-low heat until the mixture becomes fragrant and the color deepens.
  4. Add the potatoes and turn them gently through the sauce. Pour in the water or stock and add a little salt.
  5. Cover and simmer until the potatoes are tender enough to pierce easily with a fork. Stir carefully so the potatoes do not break apart completely.
  6. Uncover and reduce the sauce until it is thick enough to coat the potatoes. Taste for salt and serve with injera.
Kitchen note: Taste and adjust salt and spice gradually. Ethiopian dishes can vary by household and region, so the balance of heat, richness and acidity can be personalized.
