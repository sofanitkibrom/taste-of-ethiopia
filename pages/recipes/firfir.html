Firfir

Torn pieces of injera gently folded through a spicy onion and berbere sauce so that every piece absorbs the flavor without becoming completely mushy.

⏱ 20–30 mins
👥 3–4 servings
← All Recipes

🛒 Ingredients

  • 3–4 sheets injera, torn into bite-sized pieces
  • 3 large red onions, finely minced
  • ⅓ cup niter kibbeh or vegetable oil
  • 3 tbsp berbere
  • 1 tbsp garlic paste
  • 1 tsp fresh ginger paste
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1½ cups water or stock
  • 2 jalapeño peppers, sliced
  • Salt to taste

🍳 Step-by-Step

  1. Put the minced onions into a large skillet or pot without oil. Cook over medium heat for 7–8 minutes until their moisture reduces and the onions become soft.
  2. Add the niter kibbeh or oil and cook for another 2 minutes. Stir in garlic, ginger, berbere and tomato paste and cook gently for about 5 minutes.
  3. Pour in the water or stock and bring the sauce to a gentle simmer. Season lightly with salt.
  4. Add the torn injera a handful at a time. Fold and toss gently so the sauce coats the bread. Let it absorb the sauce for several minutes without stirring aggressively.
  5. Add the sliced jalapeños near the end. Taste and adjust the salt or spice.
  6. Serve warm, either on its own or over a fresh sheet of injera. Firfir is especially good when the injera remains soft and flavorful rather than completely breaking down.
Kitchen note: Taste and adjust salt and spice gradually. Ethiopian dishes can vary by household and region, so the balance of heat, richness and acidity can be personalized.
