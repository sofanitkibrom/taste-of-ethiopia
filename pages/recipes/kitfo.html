Meat & Spicy

Kitfo

Finely minced beef mixed with warm niter kibbeh and mitmita. This home-friendly version is fully cooked for food safety and served with classic accompaniments.

⏱ 20–25 mins
👥 3–4 servings
← All Recipes

🛒 Ingredients

  • 1 lb lean beef, finely minced
  • ⅓ cup niter kibbeh
  • 1–2 tbsp mitmita, adjusted to taste
  • ½ tsp korarima (Ethiopian cardamom)
  • Salt to taste
  • Ayib, for serving
  • Gomen, for serving
  • Injera or kocho, for serving

🍳 Step-by-Step

  1. Keep the beef cold while preparing the other ingredients. If using whole beef, trim it well and mince it very finely with a clean knife or food processor.
  2. Warm the niter kibbeh gently in a small saucepan. Do not let it smoke. Stir in the mitmita, korarima and a little salt, then remove from the heat.
  3. For a fully cooked home version, place the minced beef in a clean skillet over low to medium heat and add the seasoned butter. Stir continuously so the beef cooks evenly and absorbs the spiced butter.
  4. Continue cooking until the beef reaches a safe internal temperature. Keep the mixture moist rather than letting it fry dry; add a small spoonful of niter kibbeh if needed.
  5. Taste and adjust the spice and salt. The finished mixture should be rich, aromatic and finely textured.
  6. Serve with ayib and gomen alongside injera or kocho. The mild sides balance the heat of the spiced beef.
Kitchen note: Taste and adjust salt and spice gradually. Ethiopian dishes can vary by household and region, so the balance of heat, richness and acidity can be personalized.
