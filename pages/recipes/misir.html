Vegetarian & Spicy

Misir Wat

Creamy red lentils slowly simmered with onions, berbere, garlic and ginger. It is one of the most comforting staples on a vegetarian Ethiopian platter.

⏱ 35–45 mins
👥 4 servings
← All Recipes

🛒 Ingredients

  • 1 cup red lentils, rinsed
  • 2 large red onions, very finely minced
  • 3 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 2½ tbsp berbere, or less to taste
  • 1 tbsp garlic paste
  • 1 tbsp fresh ginger paste
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • 2½ cups warm water or vegetable stock
  • Salt to taste

🍳 Step-by-Step

  1. Rinse the lentils until the water runs mostly clear. Set them aside to drain.
  2. Place the minced onions in a heavy pot without oil. Cook over medium heat for about 10 minutes, stirring frequently, until the moisture evaporates and the onions soften.
  3. Add the oil and cook the onions for another couple of minutes. Stir in the garlic, ginger, berbere and tomato paste. Cook gently for about 5 minutes, adding a spoonful of water if needed to keep the spice paste from sticking.
  4. Add the rinsed lentils and stir until they are coated in the spice base. Pour in the warm water or stock and season lightly with salt.
  5. Bring to a gentle boil, then lower the heat. Cover and simmer for about 20 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the lentils are very soft and creamy. Add small amounts of water if the stew becomes too thick before the lentils are tender.
  6. Taste for salt and let the stew rest for a few minutes before serving. Spoon onto injera and serve with other vegetable dishes for a mixed platter.
Kitchen note: Taste and adjust salt and spice gradually. Ethiopian dishes can vary by household and region, so the balance of heat, richness and acidity can be personalized.
