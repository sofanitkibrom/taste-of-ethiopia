Tire Siga

A traditional Ethiopian beef preparation associated with very fresh beef and bold seasonings. For a home kitchen, this version uses thoroughly cooked beef for food safety.

⏱ 20–30 mins
👥 3–4 servings
← All Recipes

🛒 Ingredients

  • 1 lb lean beef, cut into bite-sized pieces
  • 1–2 tbsp mitmita or another Ethiopian spice blend
  • 1 tbsp niter kibbeh or oil
  • Salt to taste
  • Awaze or another cooked spicy sauce, for serving
  • Injera, for serving

🍳 Step-by-Step

  1. Cut the beef into small, even pieces. Keep it chilled until you are ready to cook it.
  2. Heat a heavy skillet over medium-high heat. Add the oil or niter kibbeh and sear the beef in small batches so it browns instead of steaming.
  3. Lower the heat and continue cooking until the beef is fully cooked. Stir frequently so the smaller pieces cook evenly.
  4. Season with salt and mitmita to taste. If using an awaze-style sauce, serve it on the side or toss it with the cooked beef.
  5. Let the beef rest briefly, then serve with injera and fresh vegetables or salad.
  6. For home food safety, avoid serving raw beef unless it has been prepared under appropriate professional food-safety conditions; a thoroughly cooked version is the safer choice for a home kitchen.
Kitchen note: Taste and adjust salt and spice gradually. Ethiopian dishes can vary by household and region, so the balance of heat, richness and acidity can be personalized.
