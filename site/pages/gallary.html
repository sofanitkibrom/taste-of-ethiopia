A savory, highly prized dish made of finely minced tripe, liver, kidney, and lean beef or lamb, quickly pan-fried with onions, green chilies, mitmita spice, and niter kibbeh (spiced clarified butter).

Genfo

Gizm (Ge'ezm)

A savory traditional dish made by stuffing thoroughly cleaned cow or sheep intestines (gut) with a highly spiced filling of minced meat, fat, onions, and mitmita, which is then boiled or pan-fried until tender.

Gomen

A flavorful and nutrient-rich dish of chopped collard greens slow-cooked with garlic, ginger, onions, and either oil for fasting days or niter kibbeh (spiced clarified butter).

Gomen Besega

Tender greens prepared with pieces of meat and Ethiopian seasoning.

Hibist

A thick, soft, and aromatic steamed bread often spiced with nigella seeds and prepared for special occasions.

Hilbet

A fluffy, whipped paste made from ground fava beans and lentils, served chilled with a spicy red chili sauce.

Injera

Katenga
