Genfo

A thick Ethiopian grain porridge shaped into a mound and traditionally served with a well of spiced butter in the center.

⏱ 25–35 mins
👥 3–4 servings
← All Recipes

🛒 Ingredients

  • 2 cups barley, wheat or other grain flour
  • 3–4 cups water, approximately
  • Pinch of salt
  • 2–3 tbsp niter kibbeh or butter
  • 1 tsp berbere
  • Optional honey or milk for a different serving style

🍳 Step-by-Step

  1. Bring about 3 cups of water and a pinch of salt to a simmer in a heavy saucepan. Keep a little extra water nearby for adjusting the texture.
  2. Gradually add the flour while whisking firmly. Work steadily so the flour does not form large lumps.
  3. Once the mixture becomes very thick, switch to a sturdy wooden spoon and stir vigorously. Add small splashes of water if needed.
  4. Continue cooking over low heat until the flour is fully cooked and the porridge becomes smooth, dense and cohesive.
  5. Spoon the genfo onto a plate and shape it into a mound. Use the back of a spoon to make a well in the center.
  6. Warm the niter kibbeh with berbere and pour it into the well. Serve hot, traditionally eating the porridge with the spiced butter and sharing the dish around the table.
Kitchen note: Taste and adjust salt and spice gradually. Ethiopian dishes can vary by household and region, so the balance of heat, richness and acidity can be personalized.
