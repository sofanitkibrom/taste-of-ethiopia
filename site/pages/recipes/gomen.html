Gomen

Tender collard greens cooked with onion, garlic and ginger. It is a simple, earthy side dish that fits naturally into a mixed Ethiopian platter.

⏱ 30–40 mins
👥 4 servings
← All Recipes

🛒 Ingredients

  • 2 large bunches collard greens
  • 1 large onion, finely chopped
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil or niter kibbeh
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp fresh ginger, minced
  • ½ cup water, as needed
  • Salt to taste

🍳 Step-by-Step

  1. Wash the greens thoroughly. Remove tough stems if necessary and chop the leaves into thin strips.
  2. Cook the onion in a wide pot over medium heat until soft and translucent. Add the oil or niter kibbeh and continue for another minute.
  3. Add garlic and ginger and stir until fragrant.
  4. Add the chopped greens in handfuls, turning them as they wilt. Pour in a small amount of water, cover, and cook gently until the greens are tender.
  5. Remove the lid and let excess liquid evaporate. The greens should be moist but not watery. Season with salt.
  6. Serve as a side dish with injera, alongside lentils, potatoes, cabbage or meat dishes.
Kitchen note: Taste and adjust salt and spice gradually. Ethiopian dishes can vary by household and region, so the balance of heat, richness and acidity can be personalized.
