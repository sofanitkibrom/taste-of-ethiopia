Meat & Spicy

Key Wat

A deep red beef stew in which slowly cooked onions and berbere create a rich sauce that clings to tender pieces of meat.

⏱ 60–75 mins
👥 4 servings
← All Recipes

🛒 Ingredients

  • 1½ lb beef, cut into small cubes
  • 3 large red onions, finely minced
  • ¼ cup berbere
  • ¼ cup niter kibbeh or oil
  • 1 tbsp garlic paste
  • 1 tsp ginger paste
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • 2 cups water or beef stock
  • Salt to taste

🍳 Step-by-Step

  1. Cook the minced onions slowly in a heavy pot without oil until their moisture has evaporated and the onions become soft and concentrated.
  2. Add the niter kibbeh or oil and continue cooking the onions for several minutes.
  3. Stir in garlic, ginger, berbere and tomato paste. Cook the spice paste over medium-low heat, stirring frequently. Add a splash of water whenever it begins to catch on the bottom.
  4. Add the beef and stir thoroughly so every piece is coated. Cook for several minutes, then add the stock or water.
  5. Cover and simmer gently until the beef is tender. Stir occasionally and add a little water if the sauce becomes too thick before the meat is done.
  6. Uncover near the end and reduce the sauce until it is thick and glossy. Season with salt and serve with injera.
Kitchen note: Taste and adjust salt and spice gradually. Ethiopian dishes can vary by household and region, so the balance of heat, richness and acidity can be personalized.
