Kik Alicha

A mild yellow split-pea stew flavored with turmeric, onion, garlic and ginger rather than the heat of berbere.

⏱ 45–60 mins
👥 4 servings
← All Recipes

🛒 Ingredients

  • 1½ cups yellow split peas
  • 2 large onions, finely chopped
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil or niter kibbeh
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp fresh ginger
  • 3½–4 cups water
  • Salt to taste

🍳 Step-by-Step

  1. Rinse the split peas well and pick out any stones or damaged pieces.
  2. Place the peas in a pot with enough water to cover them generously. Simmer until they begin to soften, skimming any foam from the surface.
  3. In a separate heavy pot, cook the onions with a little water until soft. Add the oil or niter kibbeh, garlic and ginger and cook gently until fragrant.
  4. Stir in the turmeric, then add the partially cooked split peas and their cooking liquid.
  5. Simmer until the peas are completely tender. Mash some of them with the back of a spoon to create a creamy texture while leaving some whole for body.
  6. Adjust the consistency with water, season with salt and serve warm with injera. Kik Alicha is a classic mild counterpoint to spicy dishes.
Kitchen note: Taste and adjust salt and spice gradually. Ethiopian dishes can vary by household and region, so the balance of heat, richness and acidity can be personalized.
