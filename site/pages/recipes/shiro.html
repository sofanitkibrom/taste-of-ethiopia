Shiro

A silky chickpea-flour stew made by whisking shiro powder into a fragrant onion and spice base until smooth and glossy.

⏱ 20–25 mins
👥 3–4 servings
← All Recipes

🛒 Ingredients

  • ½ cup shiro powder
  • 1 large red onion, very finely minced
  • ⅓ cup vegetable oil or 3 tbsp niter kibbeh
  • 1 tbsp garlic paste
  • 1 tsp fresh ginger
  • 1 tbsp berbere, optional
  • 2–2½ cups warm water
  • 1–2 jalapeños, sliced
  • Salt to taste

🍳 Step-by-Step

  1. Cook the minced onion in a heavy saucepan over medium heat without oil for about 5 minutes, stirring often, until the onion juices have reduced.
  2. Add the oil or niter kibbeh and cook the onion for another 2–3 minutes. Add garlic, ginger and optional berbere and stir until fragrant.
  3. Pour in about 2 cups of warm water and bring it to a gentle simmer. Slowly sprinkle in the shiro powder while whisking continuously. This prevents lumps and gives the stew its smooth texture.
  4. Reduce the heat to medium-low and simmer uncovered for about 8–10 minutes, stirring regularly. If the shiro becomes too thick, whisk in more warm water a little at a time.
  5. Add the sliced jalapeños near the end if you want their fresh aroma and heat to remain noticeable. Season with salt.
  6. Serve bubbling hot over injera. Shiro is excellent as one part of a larger vegetarian spread, but it can also stand on its own as a simple meal.
Kitchen note: Taste and adjust salt and spice gradually. Ethiopian dishes can vary by household and region, so the balance of heat, richness and acidity can be personalized.
