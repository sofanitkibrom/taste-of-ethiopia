Meat

Tibs

Tender beef seared at high heat, then tossed with onions, peppers, garlic, ginger and Ethiopian spices for a fast, deeply savory meal.

⏱ 20–30 mins
👥 3–4 servings
← All Recipes

🛒 Ingredients

  • 1½ lb beef sirloin, ribeye or tenderloin, cut into bite-sized cubes
  • 2 tbsp niter kibbeh or ghee
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 large red onion, sliced
  • 2 jalapeño or green chili peppers, sliced
  • 1 tbsp garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp fresh ginger, minced
  • 2 tbsp berbere spice blend
  • 2 tbsp water or stock
  • Salt to taste

🍳 Step-by-Step

  1. Pat the beef dry and cut it into evenly sized pieces. Dry meat sears better than wet meat.
  2. Heat a heavy skillet until very hot. Add the vegetable oil, then sear the beef in batches so the pan stays hot and the pieces brown instead of steaming. Remove the beef to a plate.
  3. Lower the heat to medium. Add the niter kibbeh, followed by the onion. Cook for a few minutes until the onion begins to soften while keeping some texture.
  4. Add the garlic and ginger and cook briefly until fragrant. Sprinkle in the berbere and stir quickly so it coats the aromatics without scorching.
  5. Add a small splash of water or stock and scrape the browned bits from the bottom of the pan. Return the beef and its juices to the skillet.
  6. Add the sliced peppers and toss everything together until the beef is cooked to a safe doneness and the sauce clings to the meat. Season with salt and serve immediately with injera.
Kitchen note: Taste and adjust salt and spice gradually. Ethiopian dishes can vary by household and region, so the balance of heat, richness and acidity can be personalized.
